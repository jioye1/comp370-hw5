# Complaint Type Analysis (Task 3)

## Most abundant complaint type

Across all of 2024, the most abundant complaint type is **Illegal Parking**, with 505,546 complaints. It is well ahead of the next most common types, Noise - Residential (379,255) and HEAT/HOT WATER (264,715).

## Jan-Feb vs. Jun-Jul 2024

| Borough | Jan-Feb 2024 | Jun-Jul 2024 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brooklyn | 31,702 | 32,077 | +1.2% |
| Queens | 23,255 | 25,861 | +11.2% |
| Bronx | 11,836 | 13,189 | +11.4% |
| Manhattan | 10,872 | 12,366 | +13.7% |
| Staten Island | 2,245 | 2,734 | +21.8% |
| Unspecified | 6 | 10 | n/a |
| **Total** | **79,916** | **86,237** | **+7.9%** |

Illegal Parking complaints were about 8% higher in June-July (86,237) than in January-February (79,916), an increase of 6,321 complaints. Part of the difference comes from the period lengths: Jan-Feb has 60 days and Jun-Jul has 61. Per day, the increase is about 6% (roughly 1,332 vs. 1,414 complaints per day).

Every borough had more complaints in June-July. Brooklyn and Queens have the most Illegal Parking complaints in both periods, and Brooklyn alone accounts for about 37-40% of the total. Brooklyn grew the least (+1.2%), while Staten Island grew the most (+21.8%), though from a small base. Overall, Illegal Parking is a high-volume complaint all year, with only a modest rise between winter and summer.
